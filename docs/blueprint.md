# **App Name**: StudyBuddy AI

## Core Features:

- PDF Upload and Categorization: Upload textbooks or mock exams as PDFs, categorize them (e.g., 'Chapter 1', 'Midterm Exam'), and name them accordingly.
- Initial Quiz Codification: Convert the PDF and codify the original quiz questions for initial drills.
- Adaptive Quiz Generation: Generate quizzes from uploaded PDFs. After the first attempt, regenerate questions, increasing difficulty for correctly answered questions and decreasing difficulty for incorrect ones. Heavy use of 'tool' functionality to find right level of difficulty.
- Immediate Feedback: Display correct/incorrect answers instantly after each question, along with AI-generated rationales for each choice. This feature should make heavy use of the 'tool' functionality.The rationale of why an answer was right or wrong, comes straight from the textbook or learning material in order to remain specific to the syllabus.
- Mobile-Friendly Quizzes: Ensure quizzes are accessible and functional on mobile devices.
- Performance Tracking: Track student scores across different topics/categories to identify weak areas and provide performance summaries.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: HSL (210, 60%, 50%) – A calming blue (#3399FF) to promote focus and learning.
- Background color: HSL (210, 20%, 95%) – A very light blue (#F0F8FF) for a clean, uncluttered feel.
- Accent color: HSL (240, 50%, 60%) – A vibrant violet (#735CDD) to highlight key information and interactive elements.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' sans-serif. Body font: 'PT Sans' sans-serif. 'Poppins' offers a geometric and contemporary look, used in headings. Paired with 'PT Sans' for comfortable reading of larger bodies of text.
- Use clean, minimalist icons to represent categories, file types, and actions. Icons should be easily recognizable and contribute to a neat aesthetic.
- Maintain a clean and ordered layout with clear visual hierarchy. Use whitespace effectively to avoid clutter and ensure easy navigation.
- Incorporate subtle animations for feedback and transitions (e.g., a checkmark animation when answering a question correctly). Keep animations smooth and non-distracting.
